Pick a weapon from the armory, then click a hardpoint to install it. Click a filled hardpoint to clear it, or use its × button to clear just that slot. Pick a different weapon first to swap it in place. Hardpoints alternate between two banks: even slots aim at the cursor, odd slots aim independently (V cycles REAR / MIRROR / SYNC). Firing builds heat, and heat is dangerous — armour melts, then the hull burns. Weapons marked AMMO run dry and reload; kill enemies to shake loose crates.
